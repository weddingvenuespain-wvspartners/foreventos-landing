FAQ

Before you start

How long does it take to get started?{{i:mas}}

Not long at all: we set it up for you. We import your current enquiries, connect your sales channels and configure it with you.

Can I bring my clients over from Excel?{{i:mas}}

Yes. We migrate your clients and enquiries from Excel or from your current CRM.

How do I collect the deposit?{{i:mas}}

Your client pays online by card or direct debit, through Stripe, straight from the quote. We let you know instantly.

Do I need a listing on weddingvenuesspain.com?{{i:mas}}

No. The platform works with your own channels. If you also have a listing on weddingvenuesspain.com, its enquiries come into your dashboard as one more channel.

Is there a minimum term?{{i:mas}}

No. It’s month to month and you can cancel whenever you like.
